Our Detailing Services

Exterior Detailing

Complete exterior wash and wax, paint decontamination, tire and wheel cleaning, trim restoration, and protective sealant application for a showroom shine that turns heads.

Interior Detailing

Deep vacuum, dashboard and console cleaning, door panel detailing, leather conditioning, and thorough sanitization for a fresh, clean interior you'll love.

Paint Correction

Professional multi-stage polishing to remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation, restoring your vehicle's paint to like-new condition with incredible depth and clarity.

Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting paint protection with hydrophobic properties. Guards against UV rays, chemicals, and environmental contaminants for years of protection and easy maintenance.

Upholstery Extraction

Deep cleaning extraction for fabric seats and carpets. Removes embedded dirt, tough stains, and odors using professional-grade equipment and cleaning solutions.

Mobile Detailing

We bring our professional detailing services to your location in Beloit and surrounding areas. Convenience without compromising quality or results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full detail take?

+
A complete interior and exterior detail typically takes 2-4 hours depending on the vehicle size and condition. We'll give you an estimated timeframe when you book your appointment so you can plan accordingly.

Do you offer mobile detailing services?

+
Yes! While we primarily operate from our shop in Beloit, we offer mobile detailing services within the local area for your convenience. Contact us to confirm availability for your location.

What's included in your interior detailing?

+
Our interior detailing includes thorough vacuuming of all surfaces, dashboard and console cleaning, door panel detailing, window cleaning, upholstery cleaning or leather conditioning, and complete interior sanitization. We can also add upholstery extraction for deep cleaning when needed.

How often should I get my car detailed?

+
We recommend a full detail every 3-6 months to maintain your vehicle's appearance and protect its value. Regular detailing helps prevent long-term damage from dirt, UV rays, and environmental contaminants.

Do you detail all types of vehicles?

+
Absolutely! We work on all vehicle types - from compact cars to full-size trucks and SUVs, luxury vehicles, and everything in between. No job is too big or small for our team.
